How to play

BUG WARS

A backyard turn-based strategy game. Out-economy, out-build, and out-fight a rival colony — one turn at a time.

Your turn — tap a bug, then tap a blue tile to move or a red-marked enemy to attack. Each unit acts once.
Gather — send gatherers next to Food / Mud / Honeydew. They harvest at the start of every turn while adjacent.
Build & Train — spend Mud on buildings, Food to train. Infantry > skirmisher > siege > buildings; skirmishers shoot down flyers. Walls fall to siege.
End Turn — when you're done, tap End Turn. The rival plays, then it's your go again. Destroy their nest to win. Drag to pan the map.
Play as — you'll face a random rival
