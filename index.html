How to play

BUG WARS

A backyard real-time strategy game. Out-economy, out-tech, and out-fight a rival colony.

Gather — drag-select workers, right-click Food / Mud / Honeydew to mine. They keep at it until you move them.
Build & Train — Mud builds structures, Food trains units. Counters matter: infantry > skirmisher > siege > buildings, and skirmishers shoot down flyers. Walls only fall to siege.
Research — spend Honeydew on the bottom row. Damage, armour, gather rate and a signature upgrade per colony — they compound, so tech timing is a real decision.
Command — Ctrl+1..9 saves a control group, 1..9 recalls it (tap twice to jump there). X stop · H hold · M move without engaging · Shift+right-click queues orders.
Explore — the map is bigger than the screen. WASD / arrows / screen edges / minimap to pan, mouse wheel to zoom, Space jumps to the action, F to your base.
Play as — you'll face a random rival
